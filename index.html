Hi, I’m Brad

I have been in pastoral leadership for 15 years, as an intern at various large churches of different philosophies and styles (Northpoint, Acts 29, Southern Baptist, Non-Denominational), and then I started a church in a small town in Nevada in 2014 with a small group of tenacious people wanting to reach their city and change it for the better. Over the next 10 years, the ministry grew to an established and then reproducing, multiplying church, as we baptized over 150 people, served our city in countless ways, and planted other churches, investing in the next generation of churches and leaders. I transitioned this church over to one of those leaders in 2024 and am now an executive pastor of a church of over 1000 in Reno, leading a staff of 12 with over 300 volunteers. I have led cohorts of pastors of different levels, served in churches of many sizes, and have found a ton of joy in helping other leaders think clearly about what they need to do to lead their people to accomplish their own goals and purpose in their context. After many conversations with pastors and leaders that have been helpful for them and fun for me, I began officially coaching in 2025 to use what experience I have to help other pastors and leaders lead others effectively. I’d love the chance to help you!

Not sure if coaching is for you?

I have been where you are. Most pastors don’t struggle because they lack faith or passion. They struggle because they’re carrying too much, alone, with no clear path forward.

You might be asking questions like:

  • Why does everything feel reactive instead of intentional?
  • How do I lead people well without running myself into the ground?
  • Why does growth feel harder than it should?
  • Am I actually leading—or just managing problems?

If that sounds familiar, you’re not broken. You’re just leading without a sounding board. That’s where coaching comes in.

I’m Not a Consultant. I’m a Pastor Who Coaches Pastors.

I’ve spent years in local church leadership—preaching, leading teams, building systems, navigating growth, conflict, change, and fatigue. I know:

  • What works in real churches
  • What sounds good but collapses under pressure
  • How vision, systems, and leadership actually connect

Coaching isn’t about telling you what to do. It’s about helping you see clearly, decide confidently, and lead courageously.
